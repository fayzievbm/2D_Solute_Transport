Mass-balance diagnostics for constant and variable dispersion with constant inlet concentration. Left: total stored solute, cumulative boundary input, and cumulative degradation. Right: absolute cumulative balance residual R(t), divided by cumulative input I(t) at the same physical time. R=M-M0-I+O+G, with all phases included in M and actual numerical boundary fluxes used for I and O. Outflow is retained in the ledger; its values are negligible on the plotted scale. A floor of 1e-17 is used only for logarithmic display of vanishing residual ratios. Integrated amounts retain the supplied model concentration basis per unit transverse thickness.
