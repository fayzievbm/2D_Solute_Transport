Startup-pressure correction and figure acceptance protocol
18 November 2026

The physical model, coefficients, adsorption capacities, fixed 0.04 m inlet,
ambient downstream pressure, and 15 h duration are retained. Original and
previously delivered files are not edited. All new outputs are separate.

Pressure: replace directional ADI time splitting by full-system, adaptive
implicit BDF/NDF integration. The same finite-volume spatial operator is
used. Structured linear solves are checked against independent sparse LU;
time integration is checked against an independent matrix exponential.
Pressure bounds are checked at every accepted adaptive state and every
queried dense-output state, with 1e-6 Pa roundoff allowance. Pressure is not
clipped. A separate boundary-flux time integral checks hydraulic storage.

Transport: retain the conservative two-stage ADI fluxes and accepted kinetic
increments. Use pressure at true physical midpoint/endpoints for velocities.
Use a smooth geometric startup schedule ending at 800 s, followed by the
stated 4, 2, or 1 s step. Refine the whole schedule, including startup.
Check the unchanged transport stages against an independent matrix
exponential in a prescribed steady velocity field with reactions disabled.

All four figure scenarios: constant and variable dispersion with constant
inlet, plus constant dispersion with exponential inlet rates 0.001 and
0.0005 s^-1. Do not treat the two inlet histories as dispersion variants.

Planned comparisons (all scenarios, all phases):
- Three nested meshes, levels 0, 1, 2; L=64 m, dt=2 s, pressure rtol=1e-8.
- Finest mesh dt=4 s, pressure rtol=1e-7, versus dt=2 s, rtol=1e-8.
- Level 0 time steps 4, 2, 1 s, all pressure rtol=1e-8.
- Level 0 domain length 64 versus 128 m, dt=2 s, rtol=1e-8.
- Independent finest-mesh pressure-only 15 h runs at rtol 1e-7 and 1e-8.

Acceptance targets carried forward from the prior convergence study:
- Maximum weighted relative field L2 change over x=0-4 m: temporal and
  domain <0.2%; finest spatial pair <1%. Times 0.5, 1, 2, 5, 15 h.
- Spatial final inventory, input, degradation changes <2%.
- Selected physical-point differences / finer field peak <2%.
- Full plotted histories at (0.05,0.4) m: maximum time-refinement
  difference / finer history peak <0.2%. Interpolation is disclosed.
- Decreasing changes over the three spatial levels; conditional integral
  Richardson/GCI estimates reported separately from grid-to-grid changes.
- Solute maximum cumulative residual / final input <1e-9, with every
  half-step ledger checked and saved residual/current-input also audited.
- Hydraulic storage / time-integrated boundary-flux relative residual
  after 1 s <1e-6. Pressure remains within prescribed bounds.
- Finite concentrations, no meaningful negative dissolved concentration,
  adsorption capacities respected, exact fixed inlet width, verified
  Darcy gradients and source hashes in saved outputs.

The selected figure run is mesh2_dt2_t8 only if all required checks pass.
The _t8 suffix records the use of eight Numba threads after an identical-
array speed comparison; other runs use two. Solver equations are identical.
Failure requires diagnosis/refinement, not relaxation of these thresholds.
No claim of experimental validation or independently established units is
made. Numerical agreement is not proof of an absolute discretization error.
The pressure method description in the manuscript must change accordingly.
